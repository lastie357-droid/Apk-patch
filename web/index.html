03
↓

Download and test

Get a zip-aligned, test-signed APK. Use your own signer later when you need an update-compatible release.

BRING YOUR OWN SIGNER

Need to update the original app?

Test keys are intentionally different from the publisher’s key. Download the patched APK, then use your own Android SDK signing tools locally before distribution.

LOCAL TERMINAL
zipalign -p -f 4 patched.apk aligned.apk
apksigner sign --ks my-key.jks aligned.apkAndroid signing docs ↗
